## KEY PHRASES TO LISTEN FOR (Auckland Tower 118.7 / Approach 124.3)

### Numbers Whisper gets wrong:
- "niner" → may hear "nine" or "minor"
- "tree" → may hear "three" or "free"  
- "fife" → may hear "five" or "life"
- "fow-er" → may hear "four"

### Common Tower phrases:
- "Cleared to land runway Two Tree Left / Two Tree Right / Zero Fife Left"
- "Cleared for takeoff runway..."
- "Hold short of runway..."
- "Line up and wait runway..."
- "Go around"
- "Wind (direction) degrees (speed) knots"
- "QNH wun zero (xx)"
- "Contact Approach on wun too fower decimal tree"
- "Runway vacated"
- "Number (x) follow the (aircraft type)"

### Common Approach phrases:
- "Descend to altitude (x) thousand feet"
- "Fly heading (xxx)"
- "Reduce speed to (xxx) knots"
- "Cleared ILS runway Two Tree Left"
- "Cleared RNP runway..."
- "Report established"
- "Contact Tower wun wun ait decimal seven"
- "Expect runway Two Tree Left / Zero Fife Left"
- "Traffic (x) o'clock (x) miles (aircraft type)"

### NZAA Frequencies:
- Tower: 118.700 MHz (currently recording)
- Approach/Departure: 124.300 MHz
- Ground: 121.900 MHz
- ATIS: 127.800 MHz

### Scoring tiers:
- TIER 1 (must get right): runway number, clearance type, altitude
- TIER 2 (important): QNH, squawk, frequency, heading
- TIER 3 (useful): wind, traffic info, ATIS letter
